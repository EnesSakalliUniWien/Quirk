## Actionable finding

[src/components/panels/state/useVisibleRows.js:60](/Users/berksakalli/Projects/GitHub/EnesSakalliUniWien/Quirk/src/components/panels/state/useVisibleRows.js:60) updates the slice on scrolling and resizing. The root agent's current browser inspection found 24 mounted rows for 256 states, with the range changing when scrolled, and no nearby explicit paging controls. This confirms DOM omission; it does not prove that every VoiceOver navigation strategy cannot reach the data.

Minimal recommendation: reuse native buttons for previous/next row ranges, or a labelled basis-row field, and announce the displayed range. Keep virtualization and scientific real/imaginary/probability/phase semantics. Verify actual VoiceOver traversal with both a small and a large state vector before claiming accessible completeness.
